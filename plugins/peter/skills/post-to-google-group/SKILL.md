# Post to a Google Group

Peter is president of the San Juan Pilots Association (SJPA) and regularly relays
aviation notices — airport closures, NOTAMs, construction, pilot lunches — to the
group. Google Groups has no MCP connector, so this runs through Chrome.

## Trigger

"Post this to the group", "queue up a post", "send to SJPA Announcements", or a
forwarded notice that clearly needs relaying to members.

## Steps

1. **Establish the content first.** If the source is an email, read the full
   message with the Gmail tools. Identify: what changed, effective dates, who to
   contact, and any link members need. Strip list footers, unsubscribe links, and
   "Sent from my iPhone".

2. **Confirm the target group** if Peter hasn't named one. Load
   `https://groups.google.com/my-groups` and read the list rather than guessing an
   address.

3. **Check the session.** If Chrome lands on `accounts.google.com` asking to
   verify identity, STOP. Never enter credentials. Leave the tab open, tell Peter
   to sign in, and resume once he confirms.

4. **Draft in Peter's voice.** Short, direct, factual. Lead with the operational
   impact, not the backstory. Keep dates explicit and unambiguous. Attribute the
   source when relaying someone else's notice ("Via Rick Fant / Airhawks").

5. **Show Peter the draft in chat before touching the compose box.** Subject line
   and body.

6. **Queue, don't send.** Navigate to the group, open the new-conversation
   compose view, fill subject and body. Do NOT click Post. Posting to a group is
   publishing — it needs Peter's explicit go-ahead in chat, and he should click
   the button himself or say so clearly.

7. Leave the tab open so he can review and post.

## Voice notes

- Peter signs off `-- Peter` or nothing at all on group posts.
- He does not use emoji in announcements.
- For time-critical closures, put the date range in the subject line.

## Verification

- Dates in the draft match the source document exactly — re-read rather than
  recalling them.
- The group in the compose view is the one intended.
- Nothing was posted without Peter saying so.
